How to Remove the 300,000 KRW Daily Transfer Limit (한도계좌 해제)

By Shreedevi Gyawali • Assistant Manager, Hana Bank HQ • Updated 2026

When foreign residents in South Korea open a new bank account, the bank will almost always classify it as a Financial Transaction Limit Account (금융거래 한도계좌). This caps electronic transfers and ATM withdrawals to ₩300,000 – ₩1,000,000 per day.

Why Do Korean Banks Implement This Limit?

Korean financial regulators require banks to prevent fraudulent "cannon accounts" (대포통장) used in phishing scams. To protect customers and the financial system, banks require verifiable proof of financial activity before unlocking standard transfer ceilings.

How to Successfully Lift the Restrictions

To upgrade your account to regular status (일반계좌), you need to present evidence of purpose to your bank branch:

  • Salary Account Verification: Provide 3 consecutive months of salary deposits into the account along with your Employment Certificate (재직증명서).
  • Health Insurance Payment Record: Proof of National Health Insurance (건강보험납부확인서) coverage.
  • Utility & Living Proof: Utility bill payments auto-debited (자동이체) for at least 3 months.
  • University Enrollment Certificate (재학증명서): For international students verifying active enrollment.
Pro Tip: When visiting the branch, ensure your name on all employment records matches the exact spelling on your Alien Registration Card (ARC).
