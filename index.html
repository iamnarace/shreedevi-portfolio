Introduction & About Me

A little knowledge can make a big difference.

Living in a different country comes with new experiences, opportunities, and questions. Through my content, I aim to share useful information and everyday experiences that help people better understand life in South Korea.

Born in Butwal, Nepal, I earned my BBS before moving to South Korea in 2019 to complete my MBA at Keimyung University in Daegu. Today, I serve as Assistant Manager in Foreign Trade & Remittance at Hana Bank Headquarters in Seoul, while serving the Nepali community under voice Namuna Gyawali.

Having served as Vice-President of the Society of Nepalese Students in Korea (SONSIK), I believe essential financial knowledge should be approachable, reliable, and easy to understand for everyone.
