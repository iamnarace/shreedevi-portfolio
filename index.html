About Shreedevi

Bridging Seoul’s Financial District with Global Markets

A unique combination of rigorous commercial banking operations, academic research, and community leadership.

Commercial Banking & Global Operations

I currently serve as an Assistant Manager at Hana Bank Headquarters in Seoul, where I oversee foreign trade and international remittance operations. Daily responsibilities include managing high-precision SWIFT payment messaging, processing API-based cross-border remittance integrations with overseas partner banks, executing foreign exchange operations, and conducting thorough AML/KYC sanctions screening.

With an MBA from Keimyung University in South Korea and professional experience spanning both South Korea and Nepal, I bridge regulatory compliance, banking systems, and real-world human financial needs. I am dedicated to helping foreign residents, international students, and commercial enterprises navigate complex financial frameworks with 100% confidence.

Headquarters-Level Operations: Direct experience managing institutional correspondent relationships, inward/outward liquidity transfers, and L/C trade documentation.
Bilingual & Cross-Cultural Fluency: Korean (TOPIK Level 4, KIIP Level 5 certified), English (Professional Working), Nepali (Native), and Hindi.
Regulatory Rigor: Sanctions screening, transaction monitoring, and Foreign Exchange Transactions Act compliance oversight.

My Journey: From Nepal to South Korea

My journey began in Butwal, Nepal, where I earned my Bachelor of Business Studies (BBS) and discovered my passion for finance while interning at Nepal Bank Limited. In 2019, I relocated to South Korea to pursue my Master of Business Administration (MBA) at Keimyung University in Daegu.

During my MBA, I conducted deep academic research on Women Entrepreneurship Success in Nepal, exploring how financial literacy and institutional access transform lives. Witnessing how foreign students and workers often struggle with language barriers and complex Korean administrative rules, I stepped into community leadership as Vice-President of SONSIK (Society of Nepalese Students in Korea).

Today, under my familiar social name Namuna Gyawali on TikTok (@namuna.gyawali) and Facebook, I create transparent, accessible video guides to ensure no expat in Korea gets taken advantage of or trapped by banking misinformation.

Research Focus: Women Entrepreneurship, financial inclusion, and cross-border remittances.
Community Leadership: SONSIK Vice-President (2020–2021) and Vice-Secretary (2019–2020).
Creator Mission: Providing respectful, clear, and trustworthy educational content for South Asian communities in Korea.
