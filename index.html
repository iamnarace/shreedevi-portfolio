Commercial Operations at Hana Bank Headquarters

At Hana Bank Headquarters in Seoul, my day-to-day work centers on international remittances and foreign trade operations. This includes orchestrating high-precision SWIFT messaging (MT103, MT202, MT700 series), partner bank API remittance transfers, foreign currency account management, and strict regulatory transaction monitoring.

I review Letters of Credit (L/C), bills of exchange, and commercial trade documentation under international ICC rules (UCP 600) and South Korea's Foreign Exchange Transactions Act, ensuring absolute compliance with AML/KYC sanctions screening standards.

Headquarters Desk: Inward/outward liquidity transfers and correspondent bank communication.
Regulatory Rigor: Sanctions screening, transaction monitoring, and financial crime prevention.
Global Rails: API-driven instant transfers and traditional SWIFT clearing.

From Butwal, Nepal to Seoul & Beyond

My professional path began in Nepal, where I earned my Bachelor of Business Studies (BBS) and completed my banking internship at Nepal Bank Limited. In 2019, I arrived in South Korea to pursue my Master of Business Administration (MBA) at Keimyung University in Daegu, specializing in Human Resource Management.

My master’s thesis, "Women Entrepreneurship Success in Nepal," explored how financial independence and community access create lasting social change. During university, I served as Vice-President and Vice-Secretary of SONSIK (Society of Nepalese Students in Korea), supporting hundreds of foreign students adapting to Korean culture.

Recognizing how language barriers and banking jargon regularly cause foreign residents to experience blocked accounts and remittance delays, I began sharing clear, educational video guides on TikTok as Namuna Gyawali (@namuna.gyawali) and Facebook.
